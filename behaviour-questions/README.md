## Tell me about a problem you observed and how you solved it

### Observe → Understand impact → Take initiative → Solve → Improve process

- Situation
I noticed that deployments were being done manually, which often led to delays, inconsistencies between environments, and occasional deployment-related issues.

- Task
Although deployment wasn’t fully part of my day-to-day responsibilities, I wanted to reduce risk and improve reliability by making the process more streamlined and repeatable.

- Action
I analyzed the existing deployment flow and identified manual steps that were error-prone.
I proposed integrating a CI/CD pipeline to automate build, test, and deployment steps.
I worked with the team to set up the pipeline, added basic validations, and documented the process so everyone could use it confidently.

- Result
Deployments became faster, more consistent, and less error-prone.
The team gained confidence in releasing changes, and production issues related to manual deployment reduced significantly.
This also saved time and allowed the team to focus more on development rather than release coordination.

## Give me an example of a difficult decision you had to make. What steps were involved?

### Context → Options → Trade-offs → Decision → Outcome → Learning

- Situation
During a critical release, we discovered a late-stage issue that could impact a key user flow. Fixing it properly would delay the release, while shipping as-is carried some risk.

- Task
I had to help decide whether to delay the release to fix the issue thoroughly or proceed with a controlled workaround to meet the deadline.

- Action
I gathered inputs from the team, assessed the severity and user impact, and evaluated both options.

The decision reinforced the importance of prioritizing long-term trust and quality over short-term timelines.
