# Ans STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

- I see conflict as normal and often healthy when handled well.
- My first step is to understand both perspectives by listening without taking sides. I try to understand the root cause—whether it’s a communication gap, priority mismatch, or unclear ownership.
- I then guide the discussion toward shared goals and what’s best for the team and product, not individual preferences.
- If needed, I suggest a data- or impact-based approach to decide, and once a decision is made, I ensure everyone feels heard and aligned.
- Most importantly, I focus on maintaining trust and respect, because long-term collaboration matters more than winning an argument.
